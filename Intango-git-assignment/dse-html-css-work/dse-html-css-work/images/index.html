About Umutwe Skills Centre

Founded in Kigali, Umutwe Skills Centre exists to make practical digital skills accessible to young people and job seekers across Rwanda. Our teaching is project-based: every lesson ends with something you have actually built.

Small classes, working laptops for every student, and trainers who are active professionals — that is our promise.
